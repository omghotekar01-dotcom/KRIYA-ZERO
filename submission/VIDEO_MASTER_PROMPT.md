# KRIYA ZERO — 20-Second Video Master Prompt

Create a premium, realistic, cinematic **20-second hackathon product demo video** for an Android AI application called **KRIYA ZERO — The One-Demonstration Physical Skill Compiler**.

## Format
- 16:9 landscape, 1920×1080.
- Exactly 20 seconds.
- Fast but understandable pacing.
- Realistic Android smartphone UI.
- Modern, clean startup-launch-film quality.
- Realistic Indian engineering/innovation-lab environment.
- No fantasy holograms, robots, blockchain graphics or generic AI-brain animation.

## Core message
**Show it once. KRIYA learns the procedure. Perform it again. KRIYA verifies you learned it.**

## Scene 1 — 0:00–0:03
Show a student in an engineering lab watching a normal practical-task tutorial on a phone but still being unsure whether the task is being performed correctly.

On-screen text:

**Videos can teach.**

**But can they verify?**

Transition quickly into KRIYA ZERO.

## Scene 2 — 0:03–0:07
Show an expert placing 3–4 clearly visible electronic components or colored mechanical blocks step-by-step on a tabletop/breadboard while an Android phone running **KRIYA ZERO — TEACH MODE** observes through its rear camera.

Show a clean live-camera interface with a central analysis region.

As each physical state changes, show:

- `Checkpoint 01 learned`
- `Checkpoint 02 learned`
- `Checkpoint 03 learned`

Then briefly show:

`Compiling Skill Capsule…`

Animate a clean procedure graph:

`State 1 → State 2 → State 3 → Complete`

Caption:

**One demonstration → executable procedure**

## Scene 3 — 0:07–0:12
Reset the tabletop.

A different learner tries the same procedure while KRIYA ZERO is in **VERIFY MODE**.

Step 1 is correct.

Show:

`✓ CHECKPOINT VERIFIED`

Then intentionally show the learner putting the wrong component in the wrong position or skipping a required state.

The phone detects it.

Show clearly:

`EXECUTION DIVERGENCE`

`Wrong / skipped checkpoint`

`Expected: Step 2`

`Observed: later / incorrect state`

Use a clean red warning state, not an exaggerated alarm.

## Scene 4 — 0:12–0:15
The learner corrects the physical mistake.

KRIYA observes the corrected tabletop state.

Show:

`✓ CORRECTION VERIFIED`

`Continue to Step 3`

Then:

`✓ PROCEDURE COMPLETE`

Make it visually clear that the learner did **not** need to restart after the mistake.

## Scene 5 — 0:15–0:18
Show a polished assessment screen:

**KRIYA SKILL REPORT**

- Completion: `100%`
- First-attempt accuracy: `67%`
- Sequence errors: `1`
- Corrections: `1`
- Skill Status: `VERIFIED`

Show quick use-case cards:

- Engineering Labs
- Vocational Training
- Industrial Workforce
- Field Technicians

Caption:

**Teach • Verify • Assess — on one phone**

## Scene 6 — 0:18–0:20
Finish with a premium hero shot of the Android phone displaying KRIYA ZERO beside the physical task that was just learned.

Large final text:

**KRIYA ZERO**

**The One-Demonstration Physical Skill Compiler**

Final tagline:

**SHOW IT ONCE.**

**LEARN IT ANYWHERE.**

**PROVE YOU CAN DO IT.**

Small closing line:

`Demonstration → Executable Procedure → Live Verification`

## Voiceover
Use one confident young Indian-English voice, natural and startup-professional:

> Videos can show us how a skill is done. KRIYA goes further. Demonstrate a physical task once, and the phone learns its checkpoints. When another learner performs it, KRIYA detects mistakes, verifies corrections and produces evidence of practical skill. Show it once. Learn it anywhere. Prove you can do it.

## Critical visual requirements
- One Android smartphone is the core device.
- Physical task remains simple and visually obvious.
- Use clearly different object states so the mistake is immediately visible.
- Smooth close-ups of the phone screen and tabletop.
- Modern UI using cards, progress indicators and clean typography.
- Keep `KRIYA ZERO` branding consistent.
- Camera behavior must feel physically plausible.
- Product must feel deployable today, not science fiction.
- Do not show cloud servers as required for the critical path.
- Do not portray KRIYA as magically learning arbitrary human actions.
- Focus on **structured, visually observable physical procedures**.
- No spelling mistakes in prominent UI text.

## The viewer must understand this within 20 seconds

An expert demonstrates a procedure that KRIYA has not been manually programmed for → the phone creates checkpoints → another person performs it → makes a visible mistake → KRIYA detects the mistake → the learner corrects it → KRIYA verifies completion → a practical-skill report is generated.

Final impression:

**This phone just turned a fresh human demonstration into a reusable instructor and verifier.**
